Prayer times and location

How do I set my location for prayer times and candle lighting?
  1. On Today, tap the gear to open Settings, then Location.
  2. Tap Use my location and allow “While Using the App”, or type your city and pick it from the list.

Your location is saved only on your iPhone, rounded to about 1 km. If you said no to the location prompt, you can still pick a city, or allow it later in the iPhone Settings app → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Ashrei.

The times look wrong.

Check that the city in Settings → Location is where you are; times follow that place, not your phone’s time zone. If they are still off, please send us the city, the date and the time you expected.

Widgets

How do I add an Ashrei widget?

Open Settings → Widgets in the app for an illustrated guide. In short:

  • Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose the Lock Screen, tap the box under the clock, find Ashrei and pick a widget.
  • Home Screen: touch and hold an empty spot, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Ashrei, choose a size and tap Add Widget.
  • StandBy: charge your iPhone on its side while locked, touch and hold the widgets and tap +.

Widgets are part of Premium. Open the app once after subscribing so the widgets can unlock.

My widget shows a sample or a lock.

Open Ashrei once: the widgets update when the app confirms Premium. If you have Premium on another device, use Restore purchases first (below). Widget language and Home Screen background are in Settings → Widgets.

Language

How do I change the app’s language?

Settings → App language. Ashrei is available in English, עברית, Français, Español, Русский, Português and Deutsch, and follows your iPhone’s language until you choose one. The sacred texts keep their Hebrew; the translation shown depends on what is available from an open, reviewed source in that language — otherwise the English translation is shown.

The texts

I found a mistake in a text.

Thank you! Tap Report an error on this page at the bottom of the reading page (or Settings → Report an error), choose what looks wrong and add a note. The report goes to our religious reviewer, together with the place in the text and your reading settings. Please don’t include your name or contact details. Corrections reach everyone in an app update.

Why does Settings say “Preview build”?

Every text, transliteration, blessing and calendar rule in Ashrei is checked by a qualified religious reviewer. Until that review is signed off, the app says so in Settings. Please report anything that looks wrong.

Where do the texts come from?

From public-domain and openly licensed editions (for example JPS 1917, and translations published under Creative Commons). Each one is credited in Settings → Sources & Credits.

Is Ashrei AI a rabbi?

No. Ashrei AI explains the meaning of a verse or prayer and what the classic commentators say. Answers are written by AI and can be wrong, and it doesn’t give rulings: for questions of practice, ask your rabbi.

Premium and subscriptions

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Settings → Premium and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, so we can’t cancel them for you:

  1. Open the iPhone Settings app and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions, then Ashrei.
  3. Tap Cancel Subscription (or Cancel Free Trial).

Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge. Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

What is free and what is Premium?

All sacred texts are free forever: Tehillim, the Siddur, Tanach, cycles, prayer times, reminders and Tehillim chains. Premium adds Ashrei AI, widgets, unlimited highlights, streak revive and extra share designs.
