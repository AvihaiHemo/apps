Ashrei

Tehillim, every day, wherever you live.

Ashrei brings the psalms to your iPhone with the Hebrew, a transliteration and a translation on every line, so you can say Tehillim today even if you don't read Hebrew yet.

  • All 150 psalms, with the monthly and weekly cycles
  • The Siddur in Ashkenaz, Sefard or Edot HaMizrach, with the day's insertions
  • The whole Tanach, and this week's parasha
  • Prayer times, candle lighting and gentle reminders that stay quiet on Shabbat
  • Tehillim chains to finish the book together
  • No account, no ads, no tracking

Available for iPhone on the App Store.
