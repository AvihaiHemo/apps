Paydar

Get paid for every hour you work.

Paydar is an hours tracker for hourly workers. Clock in and out in a tap, and Paydar works out what your paycheck should be — overtime, tips, breaks and differentials included — then helps you check it against your paystub.

  • One-tap clock in and out, with a Live Activity and widgets
  • Overtime, tips, differentials and guaranteed hours done right
  • Check My Paycheck: compare your paystub with what you earned
  • PDF and CSV timesheets
  • Your data stays on your iPhone; optional Cloud Backup

Available for iPhone on the App Store.
